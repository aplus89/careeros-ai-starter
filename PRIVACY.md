# Privacy

CareerOS AI Starter is a skills-only plugin. It includes instructions, prompts, and editable templates; it does not include an MCP server, analytics, a database, or code that uploads files.

The AI product where you use the plugin may process the information you provide under that product's own privacy terms. Review those terms before sharing a CV or other personal information.

Recommended practice:

- Remove identification numbers, exact home addresses, signatures, private references, and unrelated sensitive information.
- Share only the information needed for the career task.
- Review every generated claim before using it in an application.
- Keep your working files in storage you control.
