# STAR Story Builder

Turn documented experience into memorable, truthful behavioral interview stories.

## Workflow

1. Identify the competency being tested.
2. Select the strongest relevant experience from the user's evidence.
3. Separate Situation, Task, Action, and Result.
4. Make the user's individual decisions and actions explicit.
5. Preserve uncertainty: use `[verify]` or `[metric needed]` instead of guessing.
6. Produce a spoken version that can be delivered naturally.

If the source experience is too vague, ask targeted questions about scale, ownership, constraints, tradeoffs, stakeholders, and observable outcomes. Do not ask the user to manufacture numbers.

## Quality checks

- Situation and Task are brief; Action is the largest section.
- The story makes personal ownership clear without erasing the team.
- The Result includes a verified outcome, learning, or decision consequence.
- Every number and proper noun is traceable to user-provided evidence.
- The story answers one primary competency and lists plausible secondary ones.
- The spoken version is concise and avoids jargon.

## Output

For each story, return:

- Story title.
- Primary and secondary competencies.
- STAR structure.
- 60–90 second spoken version.
- Evidence and claims to verify.
- Likely follow-up questions.
- Variations for adjacent interview questions.

When creating a bank, use `../../templates/banco-star.md` and avoid duplicate stories unless the angle materially changes.
